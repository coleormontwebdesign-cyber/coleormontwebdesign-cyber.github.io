Nothing changes. You keep your exact number and your phone. When you don't pick up, the call forwards to the system and it sends the text. It's a two-minute setting on your phone, and I do it with you.

Is there a contract?

No. It's month to month, cancel anytime. And if the text-back doesn't bring you at least one job you would've lost in your first 60 days, you get your money back.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name, and the site is yours.

Will it sound like a robot?

No. It's one text, in your words, from your business: "Sorry we missed you, we're on a job. What do you need? We'll call you right back." Then you call them.

Do you use AI?

Yes, for the parts where it helps: building your site fast, and on the bigger plan, answering texts and calls you can't. But I set everything up around your business, and you're dealing with me, Cole, not a call center.

Yes. Everything happens over the phone and by text, and you see it working before it's live.
