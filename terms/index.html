How working with me works

Plain-English terms for every website I build. Updated October 2026.

What you get

  • Starter, $399. A one-page site like your free preview, hosted, with one round of changes.
  • Standard, $650–750. A full site with your photos, gallery, reviews and Google listing help, with two rounds of changes.
  • Pro, $850 and up. Everything in Standard plus online booking and any extras we agree on in writing.

Your domain name (about $12 a year) is registered in your name and is billed separately.

Paying

Half to start, half when your site is live. Payments go through Stripe. You never pay anything before you've seen your free preview and decided you want it.

Prefer to spread it out? You can pay in three parts instead: a first payment to start, and your site goes live once it's in. The rest comes in two monthly payments. Because I host your site, it may be paused if a payment is more than 7 days late, and it comes right back once you're caught up.

Timeline

Most sites go live within a week of getting your photos and details. If something on my end will take longer, I'll tell you.

Changes

Each package includes the rounds of changes listed above. A round is one list of edits sent together. Bigger changes after that are quoted before I start, so there are no surprise bills.

Refunds

Love it or it's free: if you're not happy with your site before it goes live, I'll refund your deposit in full. Once you approve the final site and it goes live, the sale is final, and I'll still make reasonable fixes for 30 days after launch.

Who owns what

Your domain, your text, your photos and your finished site belong to you. If you ever leave, I'll hand over the files. You confirm that photos and content you send me are yours to use.

Care plan

The optional care plan is $49 a month. It covers hosting, security updates and small changes. Cancel anytime by text or email, and it stops at the end of that month.

Never Miss a Job plan

$399 setup (includes your website) plus $97 a month, or $690 up front for setup and the first 3 months. It includes missed-call text-back (when you miss a call, the caller gets a text from your business), automatic Google review requests to your customers after a job, and a monthly results text. Month to month, no contract: cancel any time and you won't be charged again. Guarantee: if missed-call text-back doesn't catch at least one missed call in your first 30 days of texting, your next month is free. Your website and domain stay yours either way. Texting turns on once the phone carriers approve your business for texting, which usually takes 1 to 2 weeks; your website goes live first.

Text messaging terms

People who get texts through this service can reply STOP to stop and HELP for help. Message frequency varies, and message and data rates may apply. Review requests only go to your own customers. See the privacy page for how numbers are handled.

How I build

I use AI tools to work fast, which is how I can show you a free preview first. Every site is set up, checked and customized by me.
