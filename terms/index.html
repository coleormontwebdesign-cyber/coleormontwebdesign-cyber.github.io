How working with me works

Plain-English terms. Updated October 19, 2026.

The plans

Never Miss a Job, $97 a month. Missed-call text-back (when you miss a call, the caller gets a text from your business), automatic Google review requests after a job, a monthly results text, and a website built from your reviews.

Rebook, $197 a month. Everything above, plus "you're due" reminder texts to your past customers and AI that answers incoming texts in your business's name.

Front Desk, $297 a month. Everything above, plus an AI receptionist that answers calls you can't, takes the details and books the job. Includes up to 300 AI call minutes a month; if you regularly go over, we'll talk before anything changes.

Setup

Setup is normally $399 and covers your website, connecting the text-back to your phone, and registering your business for texting. When a setup waiver is offered (for example, starting the same day we talk), you pay only the monthly plan.

You keep your own phone number. Only calls you don't answer forward to the system. Business texting has to be approved by the phone carriers, which usually takes one to two weeks; your website and call forwarding can go live before that.

Paying and cancelling

Plans are month to month through Stripe. There's no contract: cancel any time by text or email and you won't be charged again. If a payment fails and isn't fixed within 7 days, the service may be paused until it is.

Guarantee

If the text-back doesn't bring you at least one job you would've lost in your first 60 days, you get your money back for the monthly payments made in those 60 days. A job counts when someone whose call you missed replies to the text and books. The system logs every missed call, text and reply, so we both see the same numbers. To use the guarantee, keep the text-back and your call forwarding turned on for the 60 days and ask within that time.

Texting rules

The system only texts people who called your business, customers you've done work for, and people who've given you their number. Anyone can reply STOP to never get another text. You agree not to ask me to text lists of people who haven't contacted you.

Who owns what

Your domain (registered in your name, about $12 a year), your photos, your text and your finished website belong to you. If you ever leave, I'll hand over the website files.

Just want a website?

A one-page website is $399, or a full site with a project gallery is $650. Half to start, half when it's live, and if you're not happy before it goes live, your deposit comes back in full.
