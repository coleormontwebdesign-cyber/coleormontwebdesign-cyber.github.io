Questions

Fair questions.

What if I don't like the preview?

Then you don't owe me anything. The preview is free, and you only pay the deposit if you want to move forward.

Do I own the website?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name, and the site is yours whether or not you keep the care plan.

What do you need from me?

Ten minutes to tell me about your business, plus any photos of you, your truck or your work. Phone photos are fine.

How long does it take?

Your preview is ready within 24 hours. Once you send photos and changes, your site is usually live within a week.

Do you use AI?

Yes, I use AI tools to move fast, which is how I can build you a free preview first. But I design it around your business, set up your domain and Google listing, and make every change you ask for. You're dealing with me, not a robot.

I'm not local to Massachusetts. Can you still build mine?

Yes. Everything happens over text and a quick call, and you see each version before it goes live.
