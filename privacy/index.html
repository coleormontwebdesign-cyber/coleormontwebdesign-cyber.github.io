Privacy

How I handle your information. Updated October 2026.

What I collect

Only what you give me: your name, phone number, email, business name and details about your business. That can come from a form on this site, a Facebook or Instagram form, a call, a text or an email.

How I use it

To contact you about your website, build your sample, and do the work you hire me for. That's it.

What I never do

I never sell or rent your information, and I never share it with anyone except the tools I need to do the job: Stripe for payments, and the services that host websites and deliver email and texts.

Text messages (SMS)

If you call or text Ormont Web Design or one of the businesses I set up texting for, or give your number on a form and agree to get texts, you may get text messages about your request: a reply when a call is missed, appointment and job updates, and an occasional request for a review. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop at any time, or HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third party, except the texting provider that delivers the messages.

Payments

Payments go through Stripe. I never see or store your full card number.

Your choices

Want me to stop contacting you, or delete what I have? Text or email me and I'll do it.
